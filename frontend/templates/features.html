Which features matter most?

Both models were given the same 12 features. This page shows how much each one contributed (gain importance, from the 1-hour models).

Feature importance — LightGBM vs XGBoost

What each feature means

lag_24
How many people passed in the same hour yesterday. The single most important feature.
lag_168
How many people passed in the same hour one week ago.
lag_1
How many people passed in the previous hour.
hour
Which hour of the day it is (morning and evening peaks matter).
roll_24_mean / roll_168_mean / roll_672_mean
Average count over the last day / week / month.
location_code
Which sensor — different streets have different traffic levels.
is_weekend, dow
Whether it is a weekend and which weekday.
month
Time of year.
is_public_holiday
Victorian public holiday. Important in practice, but the models learned little from it because holidays are rare and their effect is mostly absorbed by other features.
