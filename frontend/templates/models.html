How the two models compare

These are the numbers from the offline experiment on real City of Melbourne data (test period: 2026-03-01 to 2026-08-02, 344,506 hourly records).

Environment

Point forecasts (how close is the single predicted number?)

MAE = average absolute error in pedestrians per hour. Lower is better. WMAPE = error as a share of total pedestrian flow.

80% intervals (how trustworthy is the band?)

Coverage is the key number: the share of times the true count fell inside the predicted 80% band. 80% would be perfect.

Serving on a CPU-only host

Single ms = latency for one forecast request. File size = one model on disk.

Conformal calibration applied in this app

Computed on the validation block (2025-11-01 to 2026-02-28). The adjustment widens each band until coverage reaches 80%.
